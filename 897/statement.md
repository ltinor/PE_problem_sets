## **Maximal $n$-gon in a region**

Let $G(n)$ denote the largest possible area of an <strong><a title="a polygon with n sides">$n$-gon</a></strong> contained in the region $\\\{(x, y) \in \Bbb R^2: x^4 \leq y \leq 1\\\}$.<br/>
For example, $G(3) = 1$ and $G(5)\approx 1.477309771$.<br/>
Find $G(101)$ rounded to nine digits after the decimal point.
