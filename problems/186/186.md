## **Connectedness of a Network**

Here are the records from a busy telephone system with one million users:

| Telephone No. | Caller | Called |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $S_{1}$ | $S_{2}$ |
| 2 | $S_{3}$ | $S_{4}$ |
| 3 | $S_{5}$ | $S_{6}$ |
| ... | ... | ... |
| $n$ | $S_{2n-1}$ | $S_{2n}$ |
| ... | ... | ... |

The records are generated by a "Lagged Fibonacci Generator":
- For $1 \le k \le 55$, $S_k = [100003 - 200003k + 300007k^3] \pmod{1000000}$
- For $56 \le k$, $S_k = [S_{k-24} + S_{k-55}] \pmod{1000000}$

If $\text{Caller} \le \text{Called}$ then the caller is misdialed and the call fails; otherwise it is successful and connects the two people.

The Prime Minister's phone number is $524287$. After how many successful calls, not counting misdials, will $99\%$ of the users (including the PM) be a friend, or a friend of a friend etc., of the Prime Minister?

## **网络连通性**

一个拥有一百万用户的繁忙电话系统的记录如下：

| 电话编号 | 呼叫方 | 被叫方 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $S_{1}$ | $S_{2}$ |
| 2 | $S_{3}$ | $S_{4}$ |
| 3 | $S_{5}$ | $S_{6}$ |
| ... | ... | ... |
| $n$ | $S_{2n-1}$ | $S_{2n}$ |
| ... | ... | ... |

记录由"延迟斐波那契生成器"产生：
- 对于 $1 \le k \le 55$，$S_k = [100003 - 200003k + 300007k^3] \pmod{1000000}$
- 对于 $56 \le k$，$S_k = [S_{k-24} + S_{k-55}] \pmod{1000000}$

如果呼叫方 $\le$ 被叫方，则为误拨，呼叫失败；否则呼叫成功并连通双方。

首相的电话号码是 $524287$。经过多少次成功呼叫（不计误拨）后，$99\%$ 的用户（包括首相）会成为首相的朋友或朋友的朋友等？
