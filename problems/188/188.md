## **The hyperexponentiation of a number**

The hyperexponentiation or tetration of a number $a$ by a positive integer $b$, denoted by $a\uparrow\uparrow b$ or $^b a$, is recursively defined by:

$a\uparrow\uparrow1 = a$,
$a\uparrow\uparrow(k+1) = a^{(a\uparrow\uparrow k)}$.

Thus we have e.g. $3\uparrow\uparrow2 = 3^3 = 27$, hence $3\uparrow\uparrow3 = 3^{27} = 7625597484987$ and $3\uparrow\uparrow4$ is roughly $10^{3.6383346400240996 \times 10^{12}}$.

Find the last $8$ digits of $1777\uparrow\uparrow1855$.

## **数的超幂**

数 $a$ 对正整数 $b$ 的超幂（hyperexponentiation 或 tetration）记为 $a\uparrow\uparrow b$ 或 $^b a$，递归定义为：

$a\uparrow\uparrow1 = a$，
$a\uparrow\uparrow(k+1) = a^{(a\uparrow\uparrow k)}$。

因此，例如 $3\uparrow\uparrow2 = 3^3 = 27$，进而 $3\uparrow\uparrow3 = 3^{27} = 7625597484987$，而 $3\uparrow\uparrow4$ 约为 $10^{3.6383346400240996 \times 10^{12}}$。

求 $1777\uparrow\uparrow1855$ 的最后 $8$ 位数字。
