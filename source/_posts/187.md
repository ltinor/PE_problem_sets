## **Semiprimes**

A composite is a number containing at least two prime factors. For example, $15 = 3 \times 5$; $9 = 3 \times 3$; $12 = 2 \times 2 \times 3$.

There are ten composites below thirty containing precisely two, not necessarily distinct, prime factors: $4, 6, 9, 10, 14, 15, 21, 22, 25, 26$.

How many composite integers, $n < 10^8$, have precisely two, not necessarily distinct, prime factors?

***
## **半素数**

合数是至少包含两个质因数的数。例如，$15 = 3 \times 5$；$9 = 3 \times 3$；$12 = 2 \times 2 \times 3$。

在三十以下，恰好有两个质因数（不一定不同）的合数有十个：$4, 6, 9, 10, 14, 15, 21, 22, 25, 26$。

有多少个合数 $n < 10^8$ 恰好有两个（不一定不同的）质因数？
